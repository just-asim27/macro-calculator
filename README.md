# 🔥 Macro Calculator

Ever wondered how many calories you should eat, and how many grams of protein, carbs and fat that adds up to?
This app walks you through a few quick steps and gives you a full daily plan, with a protein slider so you can fine-tune it in real time.

---

## 📌 Features

- **Step-by-step flow**: a smooth slideshow, one question at a time.
- **Accurate calories**: uses the **Mifflin-St Jeor equation** for BMR, then multiplies by your activity level to get your TDEE.
- **Deficit or surplus**: choose to lose fat or gain muscle, from **0% to 30%** of your TDEE.
- **Metric and imperial**: enter height in cm or ft + in, and weight in kg or lbs.
- **Smart macros**:
  - Protein: **1.6 to 2.2 g/kg** (or **0.7 to 1.0 g/lb**, matching the unit you entered your weight in)
  - Fat: **20%** of calories
  - Carbs: **the rest**
- **Live results**: a donut chart and macro cards update instantly as you move the protein slider.
- **Mobile friendly**: works on any screen size.

## 🛠️ How to Use

1. Pick your biological sex and enter your age.
2. Enter your height and weight in the units you prefer.
3. Choose your activity level.
4. Pick **deficit** or **surplus**, then set the percentage.
5. See your daily calories and macros, and adjust the **Protein Intake** slider to see how everything changes.
